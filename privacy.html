2. Spam Act 2003 (Cth) B2B Commercial Outreach Compliance

All business-to-business (B2B) commercial electronic messages engineered by Bize Growth on behalf of our clients or our own consultancy adhere strictly to the three core pillars of the Spam Act 2003 (Cth):

  • Express or Inferred Consent (Schedule 2, Clause 4): Under the Spam Act 2003 (Cth), consent may be reasonably inferred from the conspicuous publication of a work electronic address (such as on a corporate website, public tender register, statutory register, or professional directory) where the address is not accompanied by a statement that the person does not wish to receive unsolicited commercial electronic messages, and the message is directly relevant to the recipient's business role, functions, or duties.
  • Clear & Accurate Sender Identification (Section 17): Every commercial electronic message clearly identifies the legal business name, Australian sender identity, and accurate contact details of the organisation authorising the message.
  • Functional Unsubscribe / Opt-Out Facility (Section 18): Every commercial electronic message contains a clear, functional opt-out mechanism (either via a 1-click unsubscribe header/link or direct plain-text reply instruction such as "Reply stop to opt out") honoured automatically and permanently within 5 business days (and typically instantaneously via Smartlead global blocklist suppression).
